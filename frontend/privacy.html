PRIVACY NOTICE

How inquiries and website information are handled.

Plain-language notice for the public research website and optional consulting inquiry form.

Effective date: November 23, 2026. This notice should be reviewed against the final hosting and email-provider configuration before the consulting form is activated.

Public research access

Visitors can use public research tools without registering for an account. There is no Research Pro login or payment collection at this time.

What an inquiry collects

If you submit a consulting inquiry, the form asks for your name, email, optional organization, service interest, and project description. Please do not submit medical records, patient identifiers, trade secrets, or other sensitive or confidential material through the initial inquiry form.

Why information is used

Inquiry information is intended solely to receive, evaluate, and respond to your request, discuss possible services, and maintain the associated correspondence.

How inquiry submission works

When configured, the form sends your message through a server-side email delivery provider to the site operator. The website form does not claim to store inquiries in an online customer account. Delivery depends on an administrator-configured email service; until configured, inquiries cannot be delivered and the form will explain that.

Hosting and third parties

The host and any configured email provider may process necessary request or email-delivery data according to their policies, including operational and abuse-prevention logs. External links to scientific publications and research resources lead to third-party websites governed by their own notices.

Retention and security

Correspondence may be retained as necessary to respond to the inquiry and manage the business relationship. A definitive retention schedule and deletion process should be established by the operator before production intake. No internet communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contact and requests

For questions, access, or deletion requests concerning submitted information, use the contact page. Because the inquiry service may not yet be configured, a confirmed contact email and business identity should be published before collecting real inquiries.

This notice describes the intended form design; it does not attest that a production email provider, retention policy, or compliance review is already in place.
